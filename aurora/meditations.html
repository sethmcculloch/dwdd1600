Meditations

OSHO Dynamic Meditation®

Morning practice

A fast, intense and powerful meditation designed to break old patterns in the body and mind. Through chaotic breathing, movement and stillness, it helps release suppressed energy and allows silence and awareness to arise naturally.

OSHO Kundalini Meditation®

Late afternoon / sunset practice

A meditation that begins with shaking and dancing, helping to release tensions and melt accumulated stress. As the body relaxes and energy begins to flow freely, the practice naturally leads into silence and stillness.

OSHO Nadabrahma Meditation

Any time of day

A gentle humming meditation that brings harmony between body and mind. Through sound and subtle hand movements, the practitioner becomes a witness to the inner experience, allowing peace, balance and silence to emerge.

OSHO Nataraj Meditation

Any time of day

A meditation through dance where the dancer gradually disappears and only the dance remains. When movement becomes total and effortless, a deep state of relaxed awareness arises.

OSHO Devavani Meditation

Any time of day

A soft and meditative process where spontaneous, unfamiliar sounds arise naturally. By allowing the mind to relax and expression to flow freely, the practitioner becomes an empty vessel through which energy can move.

OSHO Vipassana Meditation

Any time of day

A practice of silent witnessing and mindfulness based on observing the breath and inner experience. Through simple awareness of what is happening moment to moment, clarity and presence deepen naturally.

OSHO Gibberish Meditation

Any time of day

A playful meditation using expressive, nonsensical sounds to release accumulated mental tension. By moving beyond ordinary language, the mind clears and space opens for deep inner silence.
