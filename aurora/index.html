Welcome to Aurora Soul Balance

Aurora Soul Balance is a space for deep rest, nervous system regulation, and subtle energy work. Through Yoga Nidra, guided meditations, Reiki, and breathwork, we create gentle pathways back to your own inner balance.

Whether you’re feeling overwhelmed, creatively blocked, or simply ready to reconnect with yourself, these practices invite you to slow down, soften, and listen.

Offerings

  • Yoga Nidra: Guided journeys into conscious rest and subconscious re-patterning.
  • Meditations: Short and long-form practices to ground, soothe, and inspire.
  • Reiki: Gentle energy healing to support emotional, mental, and physical harmony.
  • Breathwork: Conscious breathing techniques to release tension and reconnect with your body.
