Yoga Nidra

Deep rest for your nervous system

Yoga Nidra, often called "yogic sleep", is a deeply restorative guided meditation that brings the body into profound relaxation while the mind remains gently aware.

During the practice, the body enters a state between waking and sleeping, allowing the nervous system to reset, release accumulated stress and restore balance.

In modern life, many people remain in a constant fight-or-flight stress response. Yoga Nidra helps activate the parasympathetic nervous system - the state where the body can rest, repair and regulate itself.

Because of its powerful effect on the nervous system, Yoga Nidra has been used in therapeutic settings to support people experiencing stress, anxiety, insomnia and PTSD.

During the session you simply lie down comfortably while being guided through a sequence of body awareness, breath and visualization practices.

Regular practice can support:

  • deep nervous system relaxation
  • better sleep
  • reduced stress and anxiety
  • emotional balance
  • greater inner clarity and presence

No previous meditation experience is needed - simply lie down, listen and allow the body to rest.

This session may be especially helpful if you

  • feel stressed, overwhelmed or mentally exhausted
  • have difficulty relaxing or falling asleep
  • notice your body holding tension or emotional stress
  • want to calm and regulate your nervous system
  • are looking for a gentle meditation practice that requires no effort

What to expect during the session

During this guided Yoga Nidra session you will lie down comfortably while being guided through a sequence of awareness practices designed to gradually relax the body and quiet the mind.

The practice typically includes:

  • gentle body awareness (body scan)
  • breath awareness
  • guided visualization
  • deep relaxation for the nervous system

Eyes remain gently closed during the practice. You may also use an eye mask to help the body relax more deeply and reduce external stimulation.
