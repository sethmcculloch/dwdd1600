Kasia Kemblowska - My Story

For as long as I can remember, I have been curious about the deeper nature of life - about consciousness, the human experience, and the invisible forces that shape our inner world.

Alongside my professional path, I completed academic studies in philosophy, which deepened my interest in the nature of existence, awareness, and the timeless questions of who we are and why we are here. Even while living a very worldly life, the spiritual dimension of reality was always quietly present for me.

My early career began in the corporate world, working as an accountant. It was a structured and logical environment, but over time I felt a strong calling toward something broader - toward experiencing the world more fully.

That calling led me to a very different path.

For the next twenty years I worked as a flight attendant for an international airline, traveling across continents and cultures. My work took me throughout Europe, Asia, Africa, the Americas, and the Caribbean. Living constantly between different worlds and cultures expanded my perspective on life in ways I could never have imagined.

Those years of travel became one of my greatest teachers. I experienced many different ways of living, many spiritual traditions, and many expressions of human connection. Being constantly in motion also created space for reflection - time to observe people, energy, and the deeper patterns of life.

During those years my interest in spirituality and inner development continued to grow. I explored meditation, yoga, Reiki, sound healing, and different approaches to understanding consciousness and human potential.

At a certain point in life, through a series of powerful life experiences, my perspective on life deepened profoundly. Those experiences opened a door to a deeper awareness of energy, consciousness, and the interconnected nature of existence. They reshaped the way I saw the world and awakened a stronger calling to follow a path that felt more aligned with my soul.

This inner shift gradually led me toward energy work and practices that support balance within the nervous system - helping the body return to a natural state of calm, presence, and inner regulation.

My journey eventually brought me to India, where I studied and received my Reiki training in Rishikesh, a place often considered the spiritual heart of yoga and meditation. Being there deepened my understanding of energy, presence, and the ancient traditions that support human awakening.

Over the years I continued expanding my practice and training through different modalities of healing and awareness. Eventually, this personal path naturally evolved into the work I share with others.

Today my work brings together several paths that have deeply influenced me:

  • Reiki energy healing
  • Yoga Nidra
  • Osho Active Meditation
  • Guided and traditional meditation practices
  • Sound healing
  • Cacao ceremonies
  • Breathwork practices facilitated by licensed breathwork practitioner Nadia Chmielinska

One of the meditation styles I love sharing is Osho Active Meditation. These dynamic practices were created specifically for modern Western minds. Instead of forcing the mind into stillness immediately, they allow the body to move, release tension and emotional pressure first - creating a natural path into silence and presence.

I have seen how powerful these methods can be for people living busy modern lives.

My work today is about creating a space where people can reconnect with their own inner balance, awareness, and natural healing intelligence.

This work is open to everyone, regardless of personal beliefs, religion, or spiritual background. The practices I share are simply tools that support people in reconnecting with themselves in their own way.

Nature also plays a very important role in my life. I currently live near Zion National Park in the United States, surrounded by powerful landscapes and vast desert spaces. Being close to nature constantly reminds me of the simplicity and wisdom of life.

Through Aurora Soul Balance, I share the practices that have supported me on my own journey - creating spaces for people to slow down, reconnect with themselves, and rediscover the quiet wisdom that already exists within them.

My name is Kasia Kemblowska, and this work is both my passion and my life path.

My Beliefs

You are pure energy
Beyond the physical body and the busy mind, there is a deeper field of awareness and energy that shapes our experience of life. When we reconnect with this inner dimension, we begin to experience more balance, clarity, and presence.
Every person has their own path
There is no single formula for inner peace or spiritual growth. Each person carries their own rhythm, their own story, and their own way of awakening. My work is not to impose a path, but to support people in discovering their own.
Healing begins with awareness
True change happens when we become aware of ourselves - our patterns, emotions, and energy. When awareness grows, transformation happens naturally.
The body holds wisdom
Our bodies carry memory, emotion, and energy. Through meditation, breath, sound, and movement we can release tension, restore balance, and reconnect with our natural state of wellbeing.
Nature is a powerful teacher
Silence, mountains, deserts, and oceans remind us of our place in the greater flow of life. When we reconnect with nature, we often reconnect with ourselves.

Nadia Chmielinska

Nadia is a certified breathwork teacher and licensed naturopath with a deep passion for holistic wellbeing.

She is currently expanding her knowledge through studies in nutrition and dietetics, deepening her understanding of how breath, nourishment and lifestyle influence both physical and emotional health.

As a mother, Nadia's own experience of motherhood inspired her to explore practices that support nervous system regulation - something many parents deeply need while navigating the demands of everyday life.

Through her personal journey with breathwork, she discovered how profoundly the breath can influence our state of mind and body. Regular breathwork practice helped her cultivate greater calm, emotional balance and vitality.

Breathwork has deeply transformed her life, and she has since been dedicated to sharing this practice with others - supporting people in reconnecting with their breath, releasing tension and creating space for greater balance and wellbeing.

In her sessions, Nadia gently guides people to reconnect with their breath and body, release accumulated stress and tension, and cultivate greater clarity, vitality and inner balance.
