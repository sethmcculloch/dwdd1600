Breathwork?

Your breath is one of the most accessible tools you have. Breathwork practices can help regulate your nervous system, release stored tension, and bring you back into the present moment.

Styles of breathwork offered

  • Gentle nervous system support: Slow, grounding practices for stress and anxiety.
  • Balancing breath: Techniques to harmonize energy and focus.
  • Somatic-focused: Breath paired with body awareness to support release and integration.

What a session looks like

Sessions begin with a brief check-in, followed by guided breathing practices tailored to your needs and capacity. You're always invited to move at your own pace and honor your limits.

Important note

Breathwork can be powerful. If you have medical or mental health conditions, please consult with a qualified healthcare professional before engaging in more activating practices.
